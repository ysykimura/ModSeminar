## タイトル
Non-commutative crepant resolutions of toric singularities with divisor class group of rank one

## アブストラクト
Non-commutative crepant resolutions (NCCRs) are non-commutative algebras giving virtual spaces of crepant resolutions of a give Gorenstein normal singularity. For toric singularities, toric NCCRs, which are NCCRs given by direct sums of divisorial modules, are of fundamental interest because they admit a combinatorial interpretation. In this talk, we show the existence and give a classification of toric NCCRs of Gorenstein toric singularities with divisor class group of rank one. They correspond bijectively to non-trivial upper sets of a certain partially ordered set. As an application of this classification, we show that all toric NCCRs are connected by iterated Iyama-Wemyss mutations.
